1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Noor Tech Apps ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application Programmer Calculator ("the App").

We are committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. Our core philosophy is simple: your calculations are your own, and your privacy is absolute.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Summary: Programmer Calculator does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or location data.

Specifically:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, IP address, or device ID.
  • 100% Local Storage: All calculation expressions, history records, and bit configuration states are stored strictly on your local device using an encrypted/sandboxed local database (Android Room/SQLite). This data never leaves your device.
  • No Cloud Sync or Remote Servers: We do not operate external servers that receive or process your mathematical calculations.
  • No Tracking / Analytics SDKs: The app contains zero telemetry, analytics, or behavioural profiling SDKs.

3. Device Permissions Used

The App requests minimal device permissions, exclusively for user experience:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback when you tap keypad buttons and bit cells. You can control or disable vibration through your device's system settings at any time.

The App does NOT request access to the internet for operation, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or location.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Programmer Calculator is completely ad-free. It does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, analytics providers (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK), or marketing trackers.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Since no personal data is collected from any user, we fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

6. Security

Because all your data is stored locally on your device within the sandboxed application environment provided by the Android Operating System, the security of your data is governed by your device's own security mechanisms (e.g., screen lock, device encryption).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

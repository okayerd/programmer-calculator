## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application **Programmer Calculator** ("the App"), developed and maintained by **Noor Tech Apps** ("we", "us", or "our").

We are dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our practices regarding user information and data security.

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
**Programmer Calculator operates 100% offline and does NOT collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal information.**

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, location data, or unique device identifiers.
- **Local Storage Only:** Any calculation history, active expressions, and bit configuration states are stored exclusively on your device using a local, sandboxed SQLite/Room database. This data is never sent to external servers.
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** Calculations are processed on-device in real-time. We maintain no remote servers or cloud accounts for your calculations.
- **No Analytics / Tracking SDKs:** The application does not contain any third-party tracking tools, analytics libraries, or behavioural profiling SDKs.

## 3. Device Permissions
The App requests only one minimal system permission:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Solely utilized to provide physical haptic feedback when tapping buttons and toggling bits on the keypad. It can be toggled on/off through your device's system settings.

The App does **not** access the internet, device storage, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

## 4. Advertising and Third-Party Services
The App is completely ad-free and does not integrate with any third-party advertising SDKs or data brokers.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)
Because our application collects zero personal data from any user, it is fully safe for all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

## 6. Data Security
All application data is isolated within the sandboxed environment managed by the Android operating system. Your device's existing security settings (such as encryption and lock screen authentication) govern the protection of your locally stored calculation history.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. Continued use of the application signifies acceptance of any modifications.
